About TMAS

A student community for healthcare.

The Texas Medical Assistant Society at The University of Texas at Austin is a student-led organization centered around education, service, leadership, and professional development.

TMAS provides students with opportunities to explore medical assisting and healthcare, connect with their peers, and build experiences that complement their academic journey.
